Before we talk

A few things
you might ask.

Is Pippa for business available today?

The B2B sales workflow is a design partner pilot proposal, built on Pippa’s existing scheduling assistant. Team approvals, CRM updates and recovery controls will be scoped and validated with pilot partners. This page describes the direction, rather than a finished enterprise product.

Which calendars and CRMs can we use?

Pippa’s current scheduling foundation uses Google Calendar. We’ll assess your CRM during discovery; HubSpot, Salesforce and Microsoft 365 are potential integration requirements, not available integrations promised by this page.

Will the agent contact customers on its own?

The proposed initial pilot requires human approval for outbound messages and calendar changes. Any later automatic actions should have explicit permissions and agreed limits.

How much does a pilot cost?

Pricing is discussed after we understand your workflow. We’ll agree on setup, the pilot period, integration work and messaging or AI usage limits before you commit.

Can we start without replacing our CRM?

That’s the intended approach. Start with one workflow around the tools your team uses. Discovery will confirm what can be connected and what remains a manual handoff.
