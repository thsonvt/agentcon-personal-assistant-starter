Before you join

A few good
questions.

Can I use Pippa today?

We’re in an invitation-based beta. Request access and we’ll email you when your invitation is ready. Existing testers can sign in.

Which calendar and email accounts work?

Google Calendar, with personal Gmail or business email hosted on Google Workspace. Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo and AOL accounts aren’t supported yet.

Do I need WhatsApp?

Approved beta users with a connected calendar can use the web assistant. WhatsApp requires additional beta setup.

Will it book things without asking?

Pippa is designed to ask for your confirmation before arranging a meeting. You can review the details before going ahead.

What happens to my data?

Read the privacy policy for what’s collected, how service providers process it, and how to request deletion. Beta signup currently opens at example.com.
